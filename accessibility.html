Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 27 September 2026

The aim is to make this website practical to use across common devices, screen sizes and assistive technologies.

Current accessibility features

The site uses responsive layouts, readable contrast, keyboard-accessible interactive service controls, labelled forms, reduced-motion support and semantic headings where practical.

Motion and media

The site includes moving media and animated reviews. Users who enable reduced-motion preferences should receive reduced animation. Important service and contact information remains available as text.

Third-party content

Some embedded services, such as Google Maps, are controlled by third parties and may have accessibility behaviour outside Nextech’s control.

Need information another way?

If you have difficulty using the website or need information in another format, contact the workshop and explain what you need.
