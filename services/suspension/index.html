What we can help with

Steering & Suspension Repairs, explained clearly.

Suspension and steering faults can show up as noises, poor ride quality, uneven tyre wear or a vehicle that no longer feels settled on the road. An inspection can identify worn or damaged components.

01

Suspension inspection

Check relevant suspension components for wear, play or damage.

02

Steering concerns

Investigate changes in steering feel, noise or vehicle response.

03

Ride-quality issues

Look into knocks, harshness or movement that feels different from normal.

04

Wear diagnosis

Identify components that need attention instead of replacing parts without inspection.

Common signs

When to get it checked.

  • Knocking or clunking over bumps
  • The vehicle feels unsettled or bouncy
  • Steering feel has changed
  • Tyres are wearing unevenly

Questions

Steering & Suspension Repairs FAQs.

Useful starting points before you book. Vehicle-specific requirements can vary, so include your make, model and symptoms when contacting the workshop.

Can worn suspension affect tyre wear?

Yes. Worn or damaged steering and suspension components can contribute to uneven tyre wear and changes in handling.

What causes knocking over bumps?

There are several possible causes across suspension and steering components, so the vehicle should be inspected rather than diagnosed from the sound alone.

Should suspension issues be checked quickly?

If the vehicle’s steering, stability or control has noticeably changed, arranging an inspection promptly is sensible.
