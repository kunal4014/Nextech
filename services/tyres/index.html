What we can help with

Tyres & Wheel Care, explained clearly.

Your tyres are the vehicle’s contact with the road. Wear, damage and changes in balance can affect comfort, grip and braking, so tyre condition is worth checking whenever something feels different.

01

Tyre condition checks

Inspect tread condition, visible damage and general tyre wear.

02

Tyre replacement

Replace tyres when required and appropriate for the vehicle.

03

Wear investigation

Look at unusual wear patterns and whether another vehicle issue may be contributing.

04

Balance concerns

Investigate vibration or wheel/tyre behaviour that may need balancing or further inspection.

Common signs

When to get it checked.

  • Tread is worn or damaged
  • The car vibrates at road speed
  • Tyres are wearing unevenly
  • Grip or braking confidence has changed

Questions

Tyres & Wheel Care FAQs.

Useful starting points before you book. Vehicle-specific requirements can vary, so include your make, model and symptoms when contacting the workshop.

How do I know when a tyre needs replacing?

Tread depth, visible damage, age and overall condition all matter. If you are unsure, have the tyres inspected.

Can uneven tyre wear point to another issue?

Yes. Uneven wear can sometimes be associated with steering, suspension or other vehicle-condition issues.

Can I ask for tyre checks during a service?

Yes. Mention any tyre wear, vibration or handling concern when booking so it can be included in the workshop discussion.
