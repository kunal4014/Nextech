Common signs

When to get it checked.

  • Squealing or grinding under braking
  • A change in pedal feel
  • Vibration while braking
  • The car takes longer to stop than usual

Questions

Brake Repairs FAQs.

Useful starting points before you book. Vehicle-specific requirements can vary, so include your make, model and symptoms when contacting the workshop.

What are common signs my brakes need checking?

Noise, vibration, changed pedal feel or reduced braking performance are all reasons to arrange an inspection.

Do noisy brakes always mean the pads need replacing?

Not necessarily. Noise can have more than one cause, so the braking system should be inspected before parts are recommended.

Can brake issues be checked during a service?

You can mention any braking concern when booking a service so the workshop can confirm the appropriate inspection.
