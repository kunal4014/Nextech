Common questions

Useful answers before you book.

Quick answers about servicing, repairs, diagnostics, pricing and visiting the Queenstown workshop.

How often should I change my oil?+

Use the service interval specified for your vehicle by the manufacturer. Nextech’s current guidance recommends checking the owner’s manual rather than applying one fixed interval to every make and model.

Do you offer logbook servicing?+

Yes. Logbook servicing is one of Nextech’s core services and is carried out to the scheduled requirements for the vehicle.

Can you diagnose a check-engine light or warning light?+

Yes. Nextech offers vehicle diagnostics and fault finding, including investigating check-engine warnings and other issues before repair work is recommended.

How do I know when my brakes need attention?+

Squealing, screeching or whining while braking can be warning signs. Changes in braking feel, vibration or unusual noises are also good reasons to have the system inspected.

Do you repair steering and suspension?+

Yes. Nextech lists steering and suspension inspection and repair among its main workshop services.

Do you service and repair transmissions?+

Yes. Transmission servicing and repair are listed among Nextech’s workshop services.

Do you service all makes and models?+

Yes. Nextech states that it works on all makes and models, covering scheduled maintenance, diagnostics and mechanical repairs.

Will I know the cost before work starts?+

Nextech’s current site describes its pricing as fair and transparent and says estimates are provided upfront. If additional work is found during inspection, confirm the scope with the workshop before proceeding.

Do I need to charge the battery after a jump-start?+

Nextech’s current guidance suggests driving for around 45 minutes after a jump-start. If the vehicle struggles to start again, the battery and charging system should be checked.

Can I book on a Saturday?+

The workshop lists Saturday opening hours of 8am–2pm. Appointment availability can vary, so send a booking request or call the workshop to confirm a time.

What if I’m not sure what service I need?+

Describe the symptoms, warning lights or noises in the booking form, or call the workshop. Nextech can inspect the vehicle and identify what needs attention.

What other repairs can Nextech help with?+

The current service list also includes general mechanical repairs, batteries and charging systems, tyres, fuel-system work, cooling-system maintenance and other workshop repairs.
