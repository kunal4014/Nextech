Common questions

Useful answers before you book.

Quick answers to the questions customers commonly ask about servicing, batteries and brakes.

How often should I change my oil?+

Follow the service interval in your vehicle owner’s manual. Nextech’s current guidance notes that the correct interval varies by make and model rather than relying on one fixed schedule for every car.

Do I need to charge the battery after a jump-start?+

Nextech’s current guidance recommends driving the vehicle for around 45 minutes after a jump-start so the charging system has time to replenish the battery.

How do I know when my brakes need attention?+

Squealing, screeching or whining when braking can be signs the braking system needs inspection. If braking feels different or makes noise, it is worth having it checked.

Do you service all makes and models?+

Yes. Nextech states that it services all makes and models, covering routine maintenance as well as mechanical repairs and diagnostics.
